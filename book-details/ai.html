Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Author: Leonard Lobel

This book introduces the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and explains how intelligent systems are designed to simulate human intelligence. It provides a clear understanding of problem-solving, reasoning, learning, and decision-making processes in machines.

The book bridges theory and practice by explaining core AI techniques along with real-world applications. It is suitable for undergraduate students who are new to AI as well as learners interested in understanding how modern intelligent systems work.

📌 Key Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and intelligent agents
  • Problem solving using search algorithms
  • Heuristic search techniques
  • Knowledge representation and logical reasoning
  • Machine learning fundamentals
  • Neural networks and deep learning basics
  • Natural language processing overview
  • Ethical and social implications of AI

🎯 Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the core principles of artificial intelligence
  • Analyze AI problems and apply suitable techniques
  • Gain foundational knowledge of machine learning models
  • Understand real-world AI applications across industries
  • Develop readiness for advanced AI and data science topics

🌍 Applications of AI

  • Healthcare diagnostics and medical imaging
  • Autonomous vehicles and robotics
  • Speech recognition and virtual assistants
  • Recommendation systems and personalization
  • Fraud detection and predictive analytics
