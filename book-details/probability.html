Probability and Statistics

Author: Prof. Kenneth Rosen

This book provides a solid mathematical foundation in Probability and Statistics, which is essential for computer science, engineering, and data-driven disciplines. It focuses on understanding uncertainty, randomness, and data analysis using well-defined mathematical principles.

The book explains probability theory, random variables, distributions, and statistical inference with clear examples and practical relevance. It helps students develop analytical thinking and apply statistical methods to real-world problems in computing and engineering.

📌 Key Topics Covered

  • Basic concepts of probability and sample spaces
  • Conditional probability and Bayes’ theorem
  • Random variables and probability distributions
  • Discrete distributions: Binomial and Poisson
  • Continuous distributions: Normal and Exponential
  • Mathematical expectation and variance
  • Correlation and regression analysis
  • Statistical inference and hypothesis testing

🎯 Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the fundamentals of probability theory
  • Analyze random events and uncertain outcomes
  • Apply statistical techniques to data analysis
  • Interpret probability distributions and results
  • Build a foundation for machine learning and data science

🌍 Applications of Probability & Statistics

  • Data analysis and data science
  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence
  • Quality control and reliability engineering
  • Risk analysis and decision-making systems
  • Scientific research and experimental analysis
